- responsibility: evidence of ownership, accountability, or decision-making
- impact: evidence that the candidate influenced people, process, or results
- reflection: evidence of learning, self-awareness, or growth from experience

Response structure subscores:
- clarity: how understandable the transcript is
- coherence: how logically ideas connect
- completeness: how fully the candidate addresses the expected questions
- relevance: how directly the candidate responds to the prompts
- conciseness: whether the response is reasonably focused without excessive repetition or drift
