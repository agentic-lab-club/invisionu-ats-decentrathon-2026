Hello, my name is Aliya and I am applying to inVision U because I really want to grow not only as student but also as person who can create something useful with other people. In university I understood that only classes are not enough if you want to develop leadership and real practical skills, so I started to look for programs where students can learn by doing. That is why this program is very interesting for me.

The program I am most interested in is leadership and social impact direction, because I care a lot about problems that students usually face but often just accept like normal thing. I want to learn how to turn concern into action, not only discuss it. For me it is important because in future I want to work in projects where I can connect organization, communication, and real benefit for people.

One big challenge I overcame was in my first year when I joined a student project team and very quickly I understood that people had motivation in words, but in practice nobody was coordinating the work. At first I was also quiet because I thought maybe I am too inexperienced to say something. But then deadline was coming and we still had confusion, so I made a simple plan with tasks, timeline, and who is responsible for what. It was not perfect and some people ignored it in beginning, but later it helped us finish the project on time. What helped me was understanding that waiting for ideal moment is sometimes worse than making one useful step.

My long term goal is to become a person who can lead education or youth-related projects, maybe in nonprofit sector or social entrepreneurship. I want to work where strategy and human meaning are both important. This program can help me because I need stronger environment, better mentorship, and communication with people who also want to build something. What motivates me in life is when I see that small organized effort can really improve situation for others.

For me leadership is not about being the loudest person in room. I think leadership is when you can bring clarity, movement, and trust, especially when people feel lost or passive. One time I showed leadership during volunteer event preparation. Two members stopped participating because they thought their work was not important. I spoke with them separately, asked what part they would feel comfortable taking, and then changed our plan a little so responsibilities were clearer. After that they became active again and event preparation became much smoother. This experience showed me that leadership is also about seeing people, not only tasks.

Yes, my family supports me. Sometimes they worry that I take too many responsibilities, but they still support my decision because they know I am serious about growth. My biggest source of encouragement is my mother, because she always tells me that confidence comes after action, not before.
