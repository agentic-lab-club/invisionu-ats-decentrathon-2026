Hi, my name is Dias and I want to join inVision U because I feel this kind of program is exactly where student can test himself in real way. I do not want only collect certificates or say that I was part of something. I want to be in place where I need to think, communicate, solve problems, and maybe fail also, because I think this is how real growth happens.

The program I am interested in most is entrepreneurship and innovation. I like this area because it is not only about business for money, but also about finding problem and making workable solution. I became interested in this after participating in one student initiative where I saw that many good ideas die not because they are bad, but because nobody structures them well. I want to improve exactly this skill.

A major challenge I overcame was leading part of a student event when one week before the date two important people left the team. At that moment I felt stressed because I understood that if nobody reorganizes work, event will fail and many people already expected it. First I wanted just to do everything myself, but then I understood this will not work. So I broke the work into smaller urgent tasks, called short meeting, and asked each remaining member to choose one responsibility they can really finish. I also took communication with speakers on myself. What helped me was staying practical and not wasting too much time on panic. In the end event happened successfully, not perfect, but good enough, and many participants gave positive feedback.

My long term goal is to build projects that can improve student opportunities, maybe in education technology or youth development. I still do not know exact final position, but I know I want to be in a role where I can combine management, problem solving, and social value. This program can help me because I need stronger tools, not only motivation. What motivates me is progress and usefulness, when I can see that work creates something beyond myself.

Leadership for me means taking responsibility for direction and energy of group, especially when situation becomes uncertain. It is also about making decisions without ignoring people. I remember one time in team work discussion people argued too long about small details and we were losing time. I suggested that first we agree on one main goal, and after that divide what is urgent and what can wait. It helped discussion become calmer and more focused. I think this was leadership because I did not just push my opinion, I helped group move.

Yes, my family supports my decision. My father is probably biggest source of encouragement because he always says that if I see opportunity to grow, I should not choose comfort too early.
